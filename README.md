# 🎯 Haryana Student Career Guide

A comprehensive, **fully offline, mobile-friendly career guidance web application** developed for 10th-standard students of Haryana. The application enables students to select their area of interest, enter their Class 10 aggregate percentage, and choose their district to receive **personalized career recommendations** — including recommended academic streams, relevant job roles, expected salary ranges, suggested government employment options, and actionable next steps.

> **Single self-contained file** — `haryana-student-guide.html` — requiring **no installation, no server, and no internet connection**. The application runs directly in any modern web browser.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎯 Career Guidance Module
- **23 career categories** encompassing a broad range of fields:
  Science, Commerce, Arts, Agriculture, Information Technology, Government Services, Defence, Sports, Medical & Healthcare, Teaching, Media & Journalism, Law, Banking & Finance, Fashion & Design, Hotel Management & Tourism, Animation & Gaming, Pharmacy, Aviation, Electrical & Electronics, Environment & Green Jobs, Logistics & Transport, Psychology & Counseling, and Beauty & Wellness.
- Students may **select a predefined interest tile** or **enter a custom interest**.
- Based on the **Class 10 percentage (range 30–100)**, the student is assigned to one of **three performance levels**:
  - **High (75% and above)** → advanced / top-tier career paths
  - **Mid (50% – 74%)** → strong mid-tier career options
  - **Low (30% – 49%)** → practical entry-level & skill-based careers
- Each recommendation includes: **recommended academic stream, three job options (with title, English equivalent, description, and salary), a suggested government job, and a clear next-step action plan.**

### 📖 Language Assistance Module
- **Hindi → English** and **English → Hindi** translations with word-by-word breakdowns.
- **Practical usage examples** provided for each translated term.
- **Explain mode**, which clarifies concepts in straightforward, accessible language.
- **"Haryana Tip"** boxes offering localized, practical advice.

### ❓ Free Question & Answer Module
- Built-in database of **over 55 questions** (Haryana-specific).
- **Keyword-based matching** instantly returns the most relevant answer.

### 📱 Fully Offline & Self-Contained Architecture
- **Zero external dependencies** — no external stylesheets, scripts, images, or fonts. All resources reside within a single file.
- Fully functional **without an internet connection**.
- **Mobile-first responsive design**, optimized for both smartphones and desktop displays.
- Includes a decorative **Haryana tricolor bar**.
- Bilingual interface combining **Hinglish and Devanagari**, reflecting the natural language of Haryana students.

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

### Online Access
Open the live link provided above to begin using the application immediately.

### Local / Offline Execution
```powershell
# Open the file directly in your default browser
start haryana-student-guide.html
```

Alternatively, drag and drop the HTML file into any web browser window. **No additional configuration is required.**

### Career Guide — Step-by-Step
1. Navigate to the **Career Guide** tab.
2. Select your area of interest, or enter a custom interest below the tiles.
3. Enter your **Class 10 percentage** (30–100).
4. Select your **district** from the list.
5. Click **"Show My Career"** — the application will present your top career options, a recommended government job, and a defined next step.
